**What it is.** Two 11-gauge steel sheet metal parts (drawings CH-01 and CH-02) for a workshop exercise. Dimensions are in inches.

**What it is for.** Sheet metal parts are cut from a flat sheet and then bent. The drawing must therefore describe the flat shape that will be cut and the bends that turn it into the final part.

**What the drawing shows.** Both sheets show **flat patterns**, with **bend notes** that give the direction (up or down), the angle and the inside radius of each bend. The edges and holes are located with **ordinate dimensioning** from a common origin, the way a CNC punching or laser cutting program reads them. **Detail views** enlarge the slots and holes. A note states that the four slots are identical, which avoids repeating dimensions.

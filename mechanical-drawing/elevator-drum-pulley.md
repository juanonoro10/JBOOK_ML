title: "Elevator Drum Pulley"
---

**What it is.** A grooved drum pulley for an elevator (drawing PAL-01), made of aluminum 6061-T6 by casting and then machining. The bore has a keyway and is dimensioned with limits (0.874–0.875 in). Dimensions are in inches.

**What it is for.** The pulley is mounted on a shaft and guides or drives the cable or belt of the elevator. The keyway transmits torque from the shaft, and the toleranced bore controls the fit between the pulley and the shaft.

**What the drawing shows.** A **full section** through the axis shows the groove profile, with its angles and depths, which cannot be read from an external view. The **limit dimensions** on the bore define the fit, and the **surface texture symbols** (63 and 32 µin) separate the machined surfaces from the as-cast ones. The notes state the material, the mass and the manufacturing route (cast, then machine).

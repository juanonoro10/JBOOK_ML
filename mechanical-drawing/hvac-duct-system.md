---
title: "HVAC Duct System"
---

**What it is.** A rectangular duct system for heating, ventilation and air conditioning (drawings HVAC-COMP-01 and HVAC-MED-01, A2 sheets). It is made of non-alloy carbon steel ducts, tees, reducers and 90° bends, with the cross section reducing along the run. The assembly is about 39.7 m long and has a total mass of about 6.1 t.

**What it is for.** The system distributes air from the main supply to several outlets. The reducers decrease the duct section after each branch, as the air flow decreases.

**What the drawing shows.** Sheet 1 is an **isometric assembly drawing** with balloons linked to a **bill of materials** of 28 items, which lists the quantity and the cut length of each straight duct. Sheet 2 is a **layout drawing** in which all positions are dimensioned from a common origin (baseline dimensioning). This avoids the accumulation of errors that chained dimensions would produce over almost 40 m.

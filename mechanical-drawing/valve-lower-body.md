title: "Valve Lower Body"
---

**What it is.** The lower body of a valve (drawing VB3-01), a gray cast iron part (ASTM A48 Class 30B, hardness 241–321 HB) with flanged connections, two of them inclined with respect to the main axis. The bore of the inner cylinder must have an N7 surface roughness, and the part is produced by casting and inspected for cracks and other defects.

**What it is for.** The body houses the internal elements of the valve and connects it to the piping through its flanges. The inclined flanges route the flow at an angle, which is common in valves where the flow path is not straight.

**What the drawing shows.** Inclined features cannot be shown in true size in the principal views, so the drawing uses **auxiliary views** (views B and C) projected perpendicular to each inclined flange. A **full section** (A-A) shows the internal cavities, and a **detail view** (D) enlarges the small features. The notes specify material, hardness, roughness, default fillet radius and the inspection required for a cast part.

title: "Caster Assembly"
---

**What it is.** A caster wheel with a 100 mm natural rubber wheel, a bent sheet metal bracket, a bronze bushing (UNS C22000), a steel post, a steel shaft and a standard M6 hex nut. It is documented as a complete set of four drawings.

**What it is for.** Casters are mounted under carts, furniture and equipment so they can be moved by hand. The post attaches the caster to the frame, the shaft is the wheel axle, and the bushing acts as a plain bearing between them.

**What the drawing shows.** This drawing set covers a full assembly documentation:

- **Sheet 1, assembly drawing:** exploded view with balloons, assembled view with overall dimensions, and **bill of materials**. The nut is a commercial part specified by its standard (ASME B18.2.4.1M), so it has no detail drawing.
- **Sheet 2, detail drawings** of the post, bushing and shaft: **limit dimensions** on the mating diameters to define the fits, surface roughness on the bearing surfaces, and the M6×1-6g thread. The three parts are grouped on one sheet because, according to the notes, they come from the same supplier.
- **Sheet 3, bracket:** flat pattern of a 13-gauge sheet metal part with bend notes.
- **Sheet 4, wheel:** section view of the rubber tread, with a note for the 35 equally spaced relief elements.

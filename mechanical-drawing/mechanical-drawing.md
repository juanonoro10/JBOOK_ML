title: "Mechanical Drawing"
---

Technical drawings prepared by me for the Mechanical Drawing course at Universidad del Norte. They were developed based on the ASME Y14.5 standard (Dimensioning and Tolerancing), in which I am certified.

Each page presents one drawing and covers a different main topic of technical drawing: what the part or system is, what it is used for, and which drawing techniques it shows. The original PDF of each drawing can be downloaded from its page.

1. **Valve Lower Body** — cast part with inclined flanges: auxiliary views, sections and casting notes.
2. **Elevator Drum Pulley** — cast and machined pulley: section view, bore fit and surface texture symbols.
3. **HVAC Duct System** — large-scale duct layout: isometric assembly, bill of materials and layout dimensions.
4. **Bevel Gear** — gear drawing with cutting data table and toleranced bore.
5. **Geometric Tolerancing** — pin and flanged hub dimensioned with GD&T: datums, concentricity, profile, runout and perpendicularity at MMC.
6. **Sheet Metal Part** — flat patterns with bend notes and ordinate dimensioning.
7. **Caster Assembly** — complete drawing set: assembly drawing, exploded view, bill of materials and detail drawings.

title: "Bevel Gear"
---

**What it is.** A straight bevel gear with 28 teeth, diametral pitch 4 and a 20° pressure angle, made of normalized steel. The bore and the keyway are dimensioned with unilateral tolerances (+0.001/0 in). Dimensions are in inches.

**What it is for.** Bevel gears transmit power between shafts whose axes intersect, usually at 90°, as in differentials and right-angle drives.

**What the drawing shows.** Gear teeth are not dimensioned one by one. Following gear drawing practice, the tooth geometry is defined in a **cutting data table** (number of teeth, diametral pitch, pressure angle, mounting distance, cutting angle, whole depth, chordal addendum and chordal thickness, face angle), and the views only give the blank geometry. The section view defines the cone and the hub, and the **toleranced bore and keyway** control the assembly on the shaft.
